# Product

## Register

brand (primary surface: marketing site). Secondary surface `portal.html` is product register (member app UI).

## Users
Two audiences.
1. Prospects and parents in the Faloppio / Como / Swiss-border area evaluating the club: tennis school for kids and adults, summer camp, court rental. Context: browsing on phone, deciding whether to visit or enroll.
2. Existing members using the reserved area: booking clay courts, joining the internal D-Match circuit, finding opponents at their level, tracking their ranking, stats and prizes. Context: quick logins from phone between matches, some longer sessions planning their week.

## Product Purpose
Redesign of sporting.co.it (Sporting Faloppio, "100% Tennis & Sport") plus a demo of a members' reserved area. The site sells the club; the portal turns casual play into a competitive, social, gamified loop (challenges, ladder ranking, matchmaking by level/day, court booking, stats and prizes) to drive retention and hype. This deliverable is a client-facing mockup/demo, not production: mock data, interactive where cheap.

## Brand Personality
Competitive, warm, club-proud. Three words: earthy, sporting, precise. It should feel like a real clay-court club with a serious internal league, not a generic SaaS or a stock-photo gym. The reserved area should feel like a game the members want to climb. Bilingual IT/EN.

## Anti-references
- Generic AI landing slop: cream/beige body background, tiny tracked uppercase eyebrows on every section, identical icon-heading-text card grids, hero-metric template.
- Editorial-typographic lane (italic display serif + mono labels + ruled columns). Not a magazine.
- Corporate-sport SaaS (navy + electric blue, dashboard clichés) for the portal.
- Cultural-symbol shortcut: Roland Garros orange / Wimbledon green-purple lifted literally. Warmth comes from clay color + type + imagery, not a borrowed palette.

## Design Principles
1. The clay is the brand. Terracotta carries identity; the surface stays cool/white so the court color reads as heat.
2. Make the ladder feel alive. Ranking, streaks, rivalries and prizes should look competitive and earned, not decorative.
3. Show the club, not stock. Lead with real court imagery and specifics (indoor/outdoor clay, D-Match, Faloppio location).
4. Motion on scroll must enhance an already-visible page, never gate content. Respect reduced-motion.
5. Mobile-first: members act from their phones courtside.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA target: body text >=4.5:1, large >=3:1. Full `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives (crossfade/instant). Keyboard-operable nav, tabs and booking grid. Bilingual IT/EN toggle persisted.
